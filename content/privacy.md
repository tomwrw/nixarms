## Cookies and accounts

The site doesn't set cookies, doesn't store anything in your browser, and has no accounts, logins or forms.

## Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this site, not from Google Fonts or another third party, so loading a page doesn't send your details to a font or image provider.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages). Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser requests a page. GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers what it does with that.

## Links to other sites

Many pages link to other sites, such as GitHub, ProtonDB and the NixOS forums. Their own privacy policies apply once you're there.
