The site counts visits with GoatCounter, an open-source analytics service. It records the page you visited, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen size, and your country (worked out from your IP address, which isn't stored). It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across other sites. The numbers are only used to see which pages are useful, never for advertising.

To stop your visits being counted, block gc.zgo.at with any content blocker, or open this link once. That saves a small "don't count me" setting in your browser. Open it again to turn counting back on.

{{- else }}

The site doesn't use any analytics or visitor tracking.
